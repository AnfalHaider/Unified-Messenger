The front desk, redrawn around the people waiting

Unified Messenger has one job for the owner of Depilex F-11, DHA-2 and Men DHA-2: show who has been left waiting, and get them answered. The last design reported that well and helped with none of it. This one is built to be worked.

This set covers every phase of the plan, from the line itself to reports, reviews, the assistant, workspaces and shipping. Each board is marked with the phase that builds it.

Every board is 1440 × 900, the size the app opens at. Customers, messages and figures are invented. Account names and locations are yours.

Theme for every board
  • The line. Everyone waiting is placed on one track by how long they have waited, one lane per location, with your reply target drawn through it. Who is late and where shows before any number is read.
  • Act on a customer, not just look. Each row opens that chat, marks it handled, or snoozes it, from the keyboard too.
  • The account page docks beside the line instead of replacing the whole window, so you never lose your place.
  • Problems with the app are kept apart from customers. Sign-ins and broken readers collect in “Needs you”, and Accounts shows every location and channel on one grid.
  • Colour means lateness and nothing else. Everything else is ink. Light, dark or match Windows, and the choice reaches every surface: title bar, sidebar, menus, scrollbars and the WhatsApp and Instagram pages themselves.
