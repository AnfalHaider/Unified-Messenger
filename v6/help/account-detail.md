# One account’s figures

How one account is doing, and whether it is being read properly.

![One account's figures](shot:account-detail)

## What it shows

- **The figures**: waiting now, the median first reply, the share within target, and how many chats the last read saw.
- **First reply, last 7 days**: the median reply per day, against your target.
- **Is it being read?**: signed in, reader working, awake. Anything wrong is said in plain words.

## Buttons

- **Open page**: the account's WhatsApp or Instagram page beside the line.
- **Read now**: reads every account straight away instead of waiting for the minute.
- **Sleep**: closes the page to save memory. It wakes when opened.
- **Reading record**: what this account's reads have been doing. See [Reading record](help:lost-login).
- **Edit**: name, location, and whether it is counted.
