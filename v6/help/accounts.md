# Accounts

Every account, arranged by location and channel, with whether each one is being read.

![Accounts](shot:accounts)

## The grid

One row per location, one column per channel. Each account shows its state:

- **Reading**: signed in and read every minute. The number is how many are waiting.
- **Asleep**: the page is closed to save memory; it wakes when you open it.
- **Sign in needed**: the page is asking for a login, so its customers are not being counted. Press **Sign in**.
- **Reader not working**: the channel's page changed and the app cannot read it. See [Channel readers](help:reader).
- **Not counted**: a personal account. Its page stays open but nobody on it is counted.
- **Open, no figures**: a channel the app cannot read yet, such as Google or Messenger.

Each account's buttons: **Open page**, **Figures** ([one account](help:account-detail)), **What happened** when it is signed out ([reading record](help:lost-login)), and **Edit**.

## Adding, editing and removing

- **Add an account**: choose the channel, give it a name and a location, then sign in on the page that opens. For "Another page", type its web address.
- **Edit**: rename it, move it to another location, or switch **Count its customers** off for a personal account.
- **Remove**, from Edit: wipes the login on this PC and deletes everything the app recorded for that account. The phone still lists this PC as a linked device until you remove it there. This cannot be undone.

## Channel readers

At the bottom, one line per channel says whether its reader is working. Click one for its [history](help:reader).
