# Assistant

A helper that runs entirely on this PC, for drafting replies and answering questions about your figures.

![Assistant](shot:assistant)

> **Not connected yet.** This screen and **Suggest a reply** beside a chat show sample text until the assistant is set up. Nothing is sent to any online service: it will run on this PC only, through a free program called Ollama.

## What it will do

- Draft a reply from a chat's last few messages, for you to edit, copy and send yourself.
- Answer questions such as "which location was slowest this week?" from the figures the app already has.
