# Morning digest

A short summary on the first opening of each day: who is still owed a reply from before closing, how many wrote since, and how yesterday went.

![The morning digest](shot:digest)

## What it shows

- **The headline**: how many customers wrote since the location closed, or since midnight when opening hours are off.
- **Still owed a reply**: customers who wrote before closing and never got an answer. Answer these first. **Open chat** goes straight to each one.
- **Since**: how many wrote while you were closed.
- **Missed calls**: calls from the last two days that nobody returned.
- **Yesterday, by location**: the share answered on time, the last 14 days as a small line, and the median reply.

**Go to the line** closes the digest. It opens by itself once a day; turn that off in [Settings](help:settings) › Notifications › **Morning digest**. You can open it any time from the search, `Ctrl K`, by typing "digest".
