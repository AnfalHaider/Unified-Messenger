# A chat beside the line

Opening a chat keeps the line on the left, puts the account's own WhatsApp or Instagram page in the middle, and shows what the app knows about the customer on the right.

![A chat open beside the line](shot:dock)

## The bar at the top

The customer, the account and how long they have waited. Then:

- **Handled** `H`: answered another way. Off the line until they write again, and the next customer opens.
- **Snooze** `S`: off the line for an hour.
- **Not a customer**: for a member of staff, the team's own number or a supplier. Never counted again, until you put it back from [Set aside](help:set-aside).
- **Reload**: loads the account's page again, if it looks stuck.
- **Close** or `Esc`: back to the line.

## The page in the middle

This is the account's real WhatsApp or Instagram page. Reply there, as you normally would. **The app never sends anything**; it only reads what the page shows.

## The customer panel

- **Seen by the app**: whether they are waiting and since when, how quickly their last few messages were answered, and when the app first saw this conversation. Only things the app actually saw.
- **Tags**: short labels such as "Regular". Press **+ Add**, type, and press `Enter`. Click a tag to remove it. Tags you have used before are offered.
- **Note**: anything worth remembering next time. It is saved when you click away, kept on this PC, and deleted if the account is removed.
- **Saved replies**: sentences you type often. Press **Copy** and paste it into the chat yourself. Write them in [Settings](help:settings) › Saved replies.

**Suggest a reply** is a preview of the assistant, which is not connected yet.
