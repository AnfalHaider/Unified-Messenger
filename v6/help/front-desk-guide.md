# For the front desk

How to work through the customers waiting, fastest first.

## The routine

1. Keep [the line](help:line) open. The customer at the top has waited longest.
2. Press `Enter`, or **Open chat**, to open that conversation beside the line.
3. Reply in the WhatsApp or Instagram page in the middle, as you always do.
4. The app sees your reply on its next read, within a minute, and the customer leaves the line by themselves. The next one is at the top.

You never need to tell the app you replied. It reads it.

## When you answered another way

- By phone or in person: **Handled** `H`. They come back if they write again.
- Need to come back to it later: **Snooze** `S`, for an hour.
- A colleague, the team's own number or a supplier: **Not a customer**. They will not be counted again.

## Useful tools beside each chat

- **Saved replies**: press **Copy**, then paste into the chat.
- **Note** and **Tags**: write down what the next person should know, such as "prefers a call back".
- **Seen by the app**: how quickly this customer was answered before.

## Keys

`J` `K` move, `Enter` opens, `H` handled, `S` snooze, `Esc` back to the line, `Ctrl K` find anyone. See [Keyboard](help:keys).
