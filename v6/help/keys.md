# Keyboard

Everything on the line can be done without the mouse.

![The search](shot:palette)

## Everywhere

- `Ctrl K`: find a customer, an account or a screen, or run an action.
- `F1`: help for the screen you are on. Press again to close it.
- `Esc`: close whatever is open on top, or leave a chat and go back to the line.

## On the line

- `J` or `↓`: next customer.
- `K` or `↑`: previous customer.
- `Enter`: open the selected chat.
- `H`: mark handled.
- `S`: snooze for an hour.

## Beside a chat

- `H`: handled, and the next customer opens.
- `S`: snoozed, and the next customer opens.
- `Esc`: back to the line.

## In the search

- `↓` and `↑` move through the results, `Enter` opens one, `Esc` closes it.
