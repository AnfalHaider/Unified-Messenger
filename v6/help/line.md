# The line

Everyone waiting for a reply across every account, longest wait first. This is the screen to keep open during the day.

![The line](shot:line)

## The headline

How many customers are waiting, how many are past your target, and how many will pass it in the next five minutes. Below it, today's measured replies: the share answered within target and the median first reply.

## The six figures

- **Waiting now**: customers waiting for a reply, with how many on each channel.
- **Past target**: how many have waited longer than your reply target, and how many more will in the next five minutes.
- **Longest wait**: the longest anyone is waiting, and who.
- **Caught up**: of the chats active today, the share that have an answer at all.
- **Answered on time**: of the replies the app has measured, the share sent within the target. It shows "—" until a reply has been measured.
- **First reply**: the median time to a first reply.

When a location is chosen in the title bar, the first three count that location only; the last three are always the whole business, and say so.

## The chart

One lane per location. Each circle is a customer, placed by how long they have waited: the dark line is your target, the shaded band just before it is the last five minutes.

- The ring's colour is how late they are: green on time, amber due within five minutes, red past target.
- The small badge on each circle is the channel: WhatsApp or Instagram.
- **Point** at a circle to see the name, the wait and the account. **Click** it to open that chat.
- Everyone waiting **more than an hour** is counted in one box at the end of the lane: how many, and how long the longest has waited. Click it to open that longest wait.
- The top of the chart and each lane's label count the waiting customers per channel.

## The list: Longest wait first

The same customers, longest wait first. The selected row shows its buttons:

- **Open chat** opens the conversation beside the line. So does double-clicking a row or pressing `Enter`.
- **Handled** takes the customer off the line when you answered them another way, by phone or in person. They come back if they write again.
- **Snooze** takes them off the line for an hour.

Rows that are not selected say how late they are: "On time", "Due in 3 min", or "2 h past target".

## Keys

- `J` and `K` move down and up the list.
- `Enter` opens the selected chat.
- `H` marks it handled, `S` snoozes it for an hour.

## What is not on the line

- Group chats, broadcasts, status updates and channels.
- Chats whose last message ended the conversation, like "ok thanks", while **Leave out chats that ended themselves** is on in Settings.
- Anyone waiting longer than the backlog limit (7 days unless changed). They are counted as backlog in [Reports](help:reports).
- Chats marked **Not a customer**, and staff or team chats caught by the rules in Settings.

All of these are listed in [Set aside](help:set-aside), with the reason.

## Needs you, and the search

- **Needs you** in the title bar lists accounts that need signing in again and channel readers that are not working, each with a button to fix it.
- `Ctrl K` finds any customer on the line, any account, any screen, and a few actions such as **Read every account now**.
