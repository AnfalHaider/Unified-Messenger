# Reading record

What an account's reads have been doing, kept so a lost login can be explained instead of guessed at.

![Reading record](shot:lost-login)

## What it shows

A timeline, newest at the bottom:

- **Good reads**, with how many chats the last one saw. Runs of the same result are shown as one line ("12 good reads").
- **Page reloaded** or **Page closed itself**: the page restarted without being asked.
- **Reader not ready**: the page was open but its data was not built yet, usually just after it loads.
- **Nothing came back**: the page answered with no chats. Figures are hidden rather than shown as zero.
- **Sign-in screen**: the login screen replaced the page.
- **Still signed out**: how long ago that was.

Only outcomes and counts are kept here: never a customer's name, number or message.

## When an account is signed out

Nobody signed it out from the app. The usual reasons:

- On WhatsApp, the phone removed this PC under **Linked devices**, or the link was not used from the phone for 14 days.
- On Instagram, the password changed or Instagram asked for a new login.

Press **Sign in again**, then scan the QR code from the phone or type the password on the page. The login is kept only on this PC.
