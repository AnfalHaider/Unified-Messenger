# For the owner

How to use the app to keep an eye on how quickly every location answers, without reading every chat yourself.

## Once

1. Add every account in [Accounts](help:accounts), each with its location.
2. Set the **reply target** and each location's **opening hours** in [Settings](help:settings).
3. Under **Not customers**, add your staff's name words and the team's own numbers, so internal chats are never counted.
4. Choose which notifications you want, and the quiet hours.

## Every morning

The [morning digest](help:digest) opens by itself. Check:

- **Still owed a reply**: anyone who wrote before closing and was not answered.
- **Missed calls** nobody returned.
- **Yesterday, by location**: which location was slow.

## During the day

- The number beside **The line** in the left strip is everyone waiting. Red in the title bar and on the line means someone is past the target.
- Pick a location in the title bar to see only that branch.
- [Needs you](help:line) lists anything the app cannot do by itself: an account to sign in again, a reader that stopped.

## Every week

- [Reports](help:reports) › **Weekly report**: save it as PDF to keep or share. Names are left out unless you switch them on.
- **Reply times** shows which accounts are slowest; **Backlog and reopened** shows who is being left for days.

## Keeping the numbers honest

- A customer answered by phone or in person can be marked **Handled**, and returns if they write again.
- Staff and team chats: **Not a customer**, or a rule in Settings.
- Everything taken off the line is listed in [Set aside](help:set-aside), with who took it off and why.
