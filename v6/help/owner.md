# Owner console

For the owner of a workspace that several PCs share: who is a member, which PCs are signed in, and removing access.

> **Not connected yet.** This screen shows sample figures, and says so, until workspaces arrive. Today each PC keeps its own accounts and figures.

## What it will do

- List the members of the workspace and the PCs each one uses.
- Remove a member: the app signs out and wipes the account logins on their PCs the next time they are online.
- Share the business setup (accounts, locations, opening hours) between PCs, without sharing any customer data.
