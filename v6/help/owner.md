# Owner console

For the person who owns Unified Messenger itself, not for a business using it. It opens only for that one Google account, from **Settings › Workspace › Owner console**.

## What it shows

- Every workspace: its name, its admins, how many members it has, when one of their PCs was last seen, and whether it is active or suspended.
- **Membership only.** No business's accounts, customers, messages or figures are in the cloud at all, and the rules refuse this account the shared setup as well.

## Suspending and restoring

- **Suspend** pauses a workspace. Every PC in it locks at its next check, which is when the app starts and every six hours while it runs.
- **Nothing is deleted.** Logins, history and the setup stay on those PCs, and the people there see a screen saying the workspace is paused, with a **Check again** button.
- **Restore** brings it back: each PC returns to normal at its next check, with everything as it was.
- A suspended workspace also stops admins changing its setup or its members.
- Suspending a workspace you are yourself a member of does not lock you out of this console.

Who may be in a workspace, and removing members, belongs to that business's own admins: see [Settings](help:settings) › Workspace.
