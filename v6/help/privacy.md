# What stays on this PC

The app reads your customer conversations so that it can count who is waiting. What it learns stays on this PC.

## What the app keeps, on this PC only

- Each account's login, in that account's own storage.
- Who is waiting and since when, and how quickly replies were sent.
- A record per day for Reports.
- Your notes, tags and saved replies.
- A log file of counts, for support. It never contains a customer's name, number or message.

## What never happens

- **Nothing is sent.** The app never sends a message, never posts a reply to a review, and never clicks anything in a chat.
- **Nothing leaves the PC.** No figures, customer names, messages or notes go to any online service. There is no tracking and no crash report sent anywhere.
- **Signing in** (Settings › Workspace) tells the app's own workspace service your Google name and email address, and nothing else. The sign-in is kept on this PC, protected by Windows so other users of the PC cannot read it. Sign out forgets it.
- **The members list** in a workspace holds each member's Google name and address, their role, and when their PC last checked in. Everyone in the workspace can see it.
- **A workspace** (Settings › Workspace) keeps the business setup: account names and pages, locations, opening hours, holidays, the reply target, saved replies and not-a-customer rules, so another PC signed in to it gets them. Never logins, messages, customers or figures, which stay on the PC that read them.

## Removing an account

Removing an account in [Accounts](help:accounts) wipes its login on this PC and deletes everything the app recorded for it, notes included. To stop the phone listing this PC, remove it on the phone too: in WhatsApp under **Linked devices**.
