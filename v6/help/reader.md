# Channel readers

Each channel, WhatsApp or Instagram, has one reader that every account on it shares. When WhatsApp or Instagram changes their page, every account on that channel fails at once, so the app reports it once, as the reader, rather than as many broken accounts.

![A channel reader](shot:reader)

## What it shows

- **What happened**: the reads across every account on the channel, newest at the bottom. When more than one account stopped at the same time, the first line says so.
- **Every reader**: each channel's state: working, working with some failed reads, or not reading.

## What to do

- **Try again now** reads every account straight away.
- A reader that is not working usually recovers by itself when the page loads properly, or needs an update to the app. The other channels keep being read either way.
- While a reader is not working, that channel's figures are hidden rather than shown as zero.
