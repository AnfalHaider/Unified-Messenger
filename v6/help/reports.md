# Reports

How the business has been answering customers, day by day. Reports follow the location chosen in the title bar, and every range can be saved as a spreadsheet.

![Reports: Overview](shot:reports)

## Choosing what to see

- **Report**: Overview, Reply times, Backlog and reopened, Missed calls, Weekly report.
- **Range**: Today, 7 days or 30 days.
- **Export**: saves the range's figures as a CSV file you can open in Excel.

Reports start from the day the app began recording. A short history says so at the top instead of showing a quiet month.

## Overview

- **Answered on time**: of the replies measured, the share within your target.
- **Median first reply** and how many replies were measured.
- **Customers who wrote**, **Waiting over a day**, **Reopened** (wrote again after an answer), **Missed calls**.
- **Answered on time, by location, day by day**, against a 90% goal.
- **When customers write**: the busiest hours of each weekday, darker is busier.

## Reply times

How long first replies took, grouped by speed, and each account's median and slowest.

![Reply times](shot:reports-reply-times)

## Backlog and reopened

Who has waited more than a day, and who wrote again after being answered.

![Backlog and reopened](shot:reports-backlog-and-reopened)

## Missed calls

Every missed WhatsApp call in the range, and whether it was returned: by message or by a call, and how long after. **Open chat** on the ones not returned.

![Missed calls](shot:reports-missed-calls)

## Weekly report

A one-page summary of a Monday-to-Sunday week, for this week or last week. Save it as **PDF**, **CSV** or copy it as an **image**. Customer names are left out unless you switch them on. It can save last week's report by itself every Monday; see [Settings](help:settings) › Notifications.

![Weekly report](shot:reports-weekly-report)
