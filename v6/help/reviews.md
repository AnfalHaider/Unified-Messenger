# Reviews

Google reviews for each location: the rating, how many reviews, and the unhappy ones still waiting for a reply.

![Reviews](shot:reviews)

> **Not connected yet.** This screen shows sample figures, and says so, until the app can read your Google profiles. The layout is final.

## What it will show

- One card per location with the rating, the total number of reviews and how they spread from five stars to one.
- The reviews that need a reply, oldest first, with the location they are for.
- A reply drafted on this PC from that review alone, to copy and post on Google yourself. The app never posts anything.
