# Reviews

Google reviews for each location: the rating, how many reviews there are, and the recent ones that still have no reply.

![Reviews](shot:reviews)

## Where the figures come from

Each location's Google Business profile is added as a **Google Business** account in [Accounts](help:accounts), and signed in once on its page. The app then reads:

- **The reviews page**, every half hour: the latest reviews Google lists, up to 50 per profile, and whether each has a reply.
- **The rating and the total**, every six hours: these come from Google Search, so the page goes there briefly and comes back.

A profile is never read while its page is open on your screen, so it never moves under you. The app only reads: it never posts or replies.

## The cards

One per location:

- **The rating**, as Google shows it beside the total, and **how many reviews** the profile has altogether.
- **The bars**: how the latest reviews spread from five stars to one.
- **Without a reply**: how many of the latest reviews have no reply yet.

"Latest 50" means Google has more reviews than the app read; the counts are about the newest ones.

## The list

- **Needs a reply** shows the reviews without one, unhappy ones first (fewest stars), then oldest.
- **All recent** shows every review read, newest first, with **Replied** or **No reply**.
- Click a review to read it in full on the right.
- **Open on Google** opens that profile's page beside the line, where you reply yourself.

A review with a rating and no words says so; the app never shows Google's placeholder as if the reviewer wrote it.

> The stars are read from the colour of each star on Google's page. If a review's stars cannot be read, it says so rather than guessing, and it is listed with the unhappy ones so it is not missed.
