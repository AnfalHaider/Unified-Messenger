# Set aside

Every customer who is still waiting but is not on the line, and why. Nothing disappears from the line without being listed here.

![Set aside](shot:set-aside)

## Why a chat is here

- **Handled**: you marked it. It returns if the customer writes again.
- **Snoozed**: you snoozed it. It returns when the hour is up.
- **Closed by rule**: the last message ended the conversation, like "ok thanks". Turn this off in [Settings](help:settings) › Look and reading › **Leave out chats that ended themselves**.
- **Not a customer**: marked from the chat, or caught by a rule for staff names or team numbers. The row says which.

## What you can do

- The buttons at the top show one kind at a time.
- **Put back** returns a chat to the line. It appears only on your own marks: Handled, Snoozed and Not a customer. A chat left out by a rule comes back when you change the rule in Settings.
- **Moved by** says whether you set it aside or a rule did, and when. **What happens next** says what would bring it back.
