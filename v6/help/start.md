# Getting started

Unified Messenger keeps every WhatsApp and Instagram account your business uses open in one window, and shows who is waiting for a reply, how long they have waited, and how quickly the team answers. It only reads. It never sends a message, and nothing it learns about your customers leaves this PC.

![The line: everyone waiting, longest first](shot:line)

## The first ten minutes

1. **Add your accounts.** Go to [Accounts](help:accounts) and press **Add an account** for each WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business or Instagram account. Give each one a name and a location, such as your branch.
2. **Sign in once.** Each account's own page opens beside the line. Scan the QR code from the phone (WhatsApp) or type the password (Instagram). The login stays on this PC.
3. **Set your target.** In [Settings](help:settings) › Look and reading, set the **reply target**: how quickly a customer should hear back. 15 minutes is the default.
4. **Tell it who is not a customer.** In the same place, under **Not customers**, add words that appear in your staff's names ("Staff", "Team") and your team's own numbers.
5. **Open the line.** [The line](help:line) is where the day's work happens.

## Finding your way

- The strip on the left moves between the main screens: **The line**, **Accounts**, **Reviews**, **Reports**, **Assistant**, **Help** and **Settings**.
- The title bar picks a **location**, shows how fresh the figures are, and opens [Needs you](help:line) and the search, `Ctrl K`.
- Press **?** in the title bar, or `F1`, on any screen to open its help beside it.

## What the colours mean

Colour only ever means lateness:

- **Green**: answered or still within the target.
- **Amber**: will pass the target within five minutes.
- **Red**: past the target.

Everything else is grey, so red always means someone is waiting too long.

> The figures come from what the app reads on each account's page, every minute. If an account is signed out, its figures are hidden rather than shown as zero, and [Needs you](help:line) says so.
