Privacy policy

In force from 20 September 2026.

Unified Messenger is a Windows app that runs on a business's own PCs. It watches the messaging accounts that business is already signed in to and shows who is waiting for a reply. This policy covers the app and these two pages. There is no other service, and no account to create with us.

What stays on the PC

All of the following is kept on the machine that read it, in the app's own folder, and is never uploaded to us or to anyone else:

Messages and customers
Conversations, customer names and numbers, previews, notes and tags.
Figures
Who is waiting, for how long, reply times, backlog, missed calls, reviews and every report.
Account logins
The signed-in sessions for WhatsApp, Instagram and Google, exactly as a browser keeps them. They never leave the PC, which is why each PC signs in to each account once.
The assistant
Optional and off until switched on. It runs on the PC through Ollama; questions, answers and drafted replies are not saved and are never sent anywhere.
The support log
Counts and timings only — never a name, a number or a message — so it can be sent to us if you ask for help.

There is no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting in the app.

What the app sends, and why

Only if you sign in with Google, and only for workspaces: a workspace lets one business share its setup between its own PCs.

Your Google name and email address
Taken from Google when you sign in, and stored in the workspace so the business's admins can see who is a member and invite people by address.
When your PC last checked in
A date and time, once a day, so admins can see which PCs are still in use.
The business setup
The names of the accounts and the pages they open, locations, opening hours, holidays, the reply target, saved replies and the "not a customer" rules. Nothing about customers, messages or figures, and never an account login.

This is held in the app's own Google Firebase project (Cloud Firestore, in India), and the rules on that database refuse anything else. People outside your workspace cannot read any of it.

Google user data

Signing in uses Google's own sign-in page in your browser. The app asks Google only for your name and email address (openid, email, profile), and uses them only to identify you to your workspace, as described above. The sign-in is kept on your PC, protected by Windows, and forgotten when you sign out.

Unified Messenger's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We do not sell this information, do not use it for advertising, and do not let humans read it except where you ask us for support, where the law requires it, or to keep the service secure.

A later version will be able to read a business's Google reviews through Google's Business Profile API, with that business's permission. Those reviews will be read and kept on the PC like everything else, and nothing about them will be uploaded. This policy will be updated before that feature is switched on.

What other services see

The app opens WhatsApp, Instagram and Google in their own windows on your PC. Your use of those sites is between you and them, under their own policies, exactly as if you opened them in a browser. We receive nothing from them. Unified Messenger is not affiliated with WhatsApp, Meta or Google.

Removing it

On the PC
Removing an account in the app wipes its login and everything recorded about it. Uninstalling and deleting the app's folder in %APPDATA%\unified-messenger-v6 removes the rest.
From a workspace
Sign out in Settings › Workspace, or ask an admin to remove you: at that PC's next check it signs out and wipes the logins it had from the workspace. To have your member entry deleted from the workspace altogether, write to us at the address below and we will remove it.

These pages

The site is served by Firebase Hosting, which keeps standard web-server request logs. There are no cookies, no analytics and no trackers on either page.
